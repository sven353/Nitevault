The Eco-Hybrid Model

Hardware you keep for years. A pouch you don't.

The straw collar, clamp, buckle, and carabiners are built to last, no yearly upgrade cycle. The only part you ever replace is the bio-film pouch insert, designed to be compostable rather than washed forever like a bottle. It's the part of "eco" that's actually easy to keep doing.

  • Silicone cup covers stop tampering but don't hold, carry, or refill a drink at all.
  • Bulky hydration packs solve carry but say nothing about drink safety or waste.
  • Nitevault sits across both categories at once: sealed, wearable, and designed to leave less behind.
